# Agent 5 — Cybernetic & Control-Theoretic Approaches to AI Safety: Source List

**This lane requires the most digging — sources are scattered and often outside mainstream AI venues. Surface obscure pieces if they're substantive. That is the entire point of this lane.**

Cover classical and modern control theory applied to AI safety, cybernetics as a framework for AI governance, and the "AI control" research agenda distinct from alignment.

## Primary focus areas

- Classical cybernetics applied to AI systems: Norbert Wiener, W. Ross Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety, the Good Regulator Theorem (Conant-Ashby), applied to AI governance
- Control-theoretic ML: Lyapunov stability, reachability analysis, viability theory applied to AI behavior bounds
- Safe interruptibility: the off-switch problem, shutdown-ability in deployed AI systems
- The "AI control" agenda (Redwood Research's framing) — control as distinct from alignment: can we build systems that remain safe even if the model is deceptive?
- Population-level governance: aligning a population of deployed models, not just individual instances
- Feedback and closed-loop control of AI systems: runtime behavioral monitoring as a control signal
- Distributed AI control: control-theoretic approaches to multi-agent systems, stability of agent networks
- Viability kernels and constraint satisfaction: keeping AI behavior within acceptable bounds under uncertainty
- Mechanism design for AI safety: game-theoretic and mechanism design approaches to ensuring safe AI behavior
