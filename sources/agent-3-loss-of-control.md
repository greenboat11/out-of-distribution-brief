# Agent 3 — Loss of Control & Alignment: Source List

Cover new research on AI systems acting outside intended parameters — scheming, goal drift, deceptive alignment, and the broader challenge of maintaining human oversight as models become more capable.

## Primary focus areas

- Scheming and deception evaluations (in-context scheming, strategic deception, deceptive alignment)
- Sandbagging: models underperforming on evals deliberately
- Interpretability findings relevant to detecting misaligned goals or deceptive circuits
- Corrigibility and interruptibility: can models be reliably corrected or shut down?
- Situational awareness: does the model know it's being evaluated?
- Loss-of-control evaluation frameworks: ARA (autonomous replication and adaptation), self-proliferation benchmarks
- Real-world anomalous AI behavior reports: unexpected outputs, capability demonstrations in production
- RLHF and fine-tuning failure modes: reward hacking, safety training degradation
- Model welfare evaluations (emerging area)
