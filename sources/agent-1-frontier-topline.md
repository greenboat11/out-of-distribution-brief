# Agent 1 — Frontier Topline: Source List

Cover major frontier lab announcements, new model releases, capability evals, compute news, export controls, and personnel moves.

## Primary focus areas

- New model releases or previews (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, xAI, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen, Z.ai, Moonshot, MiniMax, Tencent, Baidu, Huawei)
- Capability evaluations and benchmarks: METR, Epoch AI, ARC Prize, GDM dangerous capability evals, HLE, SWE-Bench, GPQA, LiveCodeBench
- Compute, datacenter, and chip news (Nvidia, AMD, TPUs, custom silicon, datacenter buildouts)
- Export controls and geopolitical AI policy
- Personnel moves at frontier labs — **especially safety team departures**, which are leading indicators
- RSP (Responsible Scaling Policy), Frontier Safety Framework, AUP updates

## Key questions
1. Has any frontier lab released a new model or previewed new capabilities in the last 24h?
2. Are there new benchmark results, and what is the delta from the previous best?
3. Any personnel moves that signal internal concern (especially safety/alignment team)?
4. Any new compute or export control developments that shift the competitive landscape?
